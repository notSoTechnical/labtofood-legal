What this app is — and is not

Lab To Food AI turns lab-report values into plain-language explanations and everyday food guidance. It is an educational wellness tool only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease, it does not replace your doctor, and nothing in the app is medical advice. Always discuss your results — and any diet, supplement, or medication change — with your own doctor. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

Estimates

Meal nutrition values (from photos, text descriptions, or barcodes) are estimates, not measurements; they can be wrong, and you can edit them. Daily calorie and macro targets from the goal quiz use the standard Mifflin-St Jeor formula and are general wellness estimates, not a prescription. When a lab marker sets a cap for a nutrient, the app shows the lab limit as the governing one.

Your content

You may only upload lab reports and photos that are yours or that you have permission to use (for example, a family member who asked you to). You keep all rights to your content. By uploading a photo for analysis you grant us the limited permission to process it once, as described in the Privacy Policy; the original photo is deleted from our server right after analysis.

Streaks and the Health Star

Streaks and the monthly "Health Star" celebrate effort — days you logged meals — never lab values, weight, or health outcomes. They are a family celebration feature only: no prizes, rewards, or payments are promised or owed for them.

Barcode product data comes from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License (ODbL). We are not affiliated with Open Food Facts, and product data may be incomplete or inaccurate.
