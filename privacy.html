Lab To Food AI — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

The short version

Lab To Food AI has no accounts and no names. Profiles use nicknames only. Your lab values, meal logs, goal-quiz answers, weight entries and streaks are stored on your device. Photos you choose are sent to our server only to be analyzed once — the original photo is deleted from our server as soon as the analysis is done — and are never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train models by us.

What the app stores on your device

  • Nickname profiles (a nickname, an emoji, a language, daily caps you set). There is no field for a real name, date of birth, address, or insurance number anywhere in the app.
  • Lab results as structured values only (marker name, value, unit, reference range) extracted from reports you upload.
  • Meal logs, daily intake totals, goal-quiz answers, weight entries, logging streaks, and your free-tier usage counters.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also clear a profile's saved report inside the app at any time.

Photos you choose to analyze

  • Lab report photos: the image is transmitted over HTTPS to our backend, which passes it to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to extract the printed values. The original image is deleted right after extraction — on the server it exists only for the duration of the analysis request. Only the extracted values return to, and are kept on, your device.
  • Meal photos: same one-time flow — sent for a nutrition estimate, then deleted from our server. The estimate (not the photo) is what the app keeps.
  • Barcodes: only the barcode number is sent; product data comes from the public Open Food Facts database. No photo is involved.
  • Before uploading a lab report, the app asks you to cover personal details (name, date of birth, address, record numbers) with a marker pen. Please do — the values are all the app needs.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no analytics SDKs, no data selling — ever.
  • No diagnosis. Lab To Food AI is an educational wellness tool; it explains values in plain language and never tells you that you have a condition.
  • We are not a healthcare provider and this app is not a medical device. We do not claim HIPAA compliance; instead, the app is designed so that identifying health records never need to leave your phone in the first place.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store and managed for us by RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (which plan, whether it is active) — never your card details. Purchases are linked to your Apple ID by Apple and RevenueCat, not to any account in this app, because the app has no accounts.

Children

Lab To Food AI is not directed at children under 13, and profiles are meant to be created and managed by adults (typically for their family).

Your choices & contact

Everything the app knows lives on your device: remove it by deleting a profile's data in the app or deleting the app itself. Questions or requests: info@annizon.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
